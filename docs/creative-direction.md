# Heather Newbuild Cottage: creative direction notes

Working notes for editing @heather_newbuild_cottage TikToks. The full brand
guide was supplied in conversation; this file records the essentials plus what
has been learned from reviewing real footage and feedback.

## Non-negotiables

- No stylised filters, grain, vignettes or lighting changes. A very gentle
  soft, warm grade is welcome if paint colours and wood tones stay true;
  avoid excess yellow, orange, contrast or saturation.
- No zooms, flashy transitions or effects added for the sake of looking edited.
- No cheesy, generic or "AI-sounding" text. Fewer words is better; no text
  is often best.
- Never state details that cannot be verified from footage (paint names,
  techniques, history of a piece). Ask instead.
- Analyse the footage before editing: best shots, textures, colours, light,
  story.
- Export 1080x1920 from the original clips, never from a TikTok download
  (compressed, and any burned-in text cannot be removed).

## Taste, as seen in the footage

- Light: low, raking golden sunlight across wood grain is the most beautiful
  light in the home. Shots with it deserve the most screen time.
- Textures: bare pine grain, sawdust, brush bristles, turned legs, painted
  spindles.
- Colours that sing: warm putty/cream paint, honey pine, the dark heritage
  green kitchen cabinets, dark wood floor. Bright yellow sugar soap and red
  heart sponges are branded props: show them briefly, don't linger.
- Story shape that works: cold grey-painted pine -> stripped back -> warm
  putty and honey pine. The colour change itself is the story.
- Hands at work (painting, brushing, sanding) feel authentic and personal.
- A second, smaller reveal (e.g. a matching chair) makes a satisfying end.

## Text overlays

- Cormorant Garamond (Medium, upright) is the house typeface. One delicate
  serif only, small, cream or mushroom depending on the
  background; placed clear of the subject and the TikTok interface.
- Prefer useful, quiet text (e.g. the paint colour on the painting shot)
  over slogans.
- Rejected: "a soft, chalky trim paint" (inaccurate), "and here she is...
  ready for tea & cake" (cheesy).
